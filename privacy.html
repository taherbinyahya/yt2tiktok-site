Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

yt2tiktok is a personal tool used by the owner of the Anasheed Al-Rahma channel to copy videos from their own YouTube channel to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube: read-only access to the list of videos on the owner's channel (titles, descriptions, durations, visibility).
  • TikTok: basic profile information and permission to upload videos to the owner's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner a list of their videos and to upload the videos they select to their TikTok account. Nothing else is done with it.

Storage

Login tokens and downloaded video files are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than YouTube's and TikTok's own services.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account settings (Security → Third-party apps) and from TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services).

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Anasheed Al-Rahma YouTube channel.
